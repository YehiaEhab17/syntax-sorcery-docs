## EXP/Levels

to keep the game fresh / a feeling of getting stronger or making progress, maybe specific types of syntax are locked behind levels.

E.g. at level 1 you have access to basic syntax such as prints

at a later level you unlock conditionals (if, switch, match etc)

Later you can unlock loops

Maybe certain data types locked behind levels. E.g. at the beginning you only have Shorts, later you unlock ints and floats.

As you unlock more "skills" challenges get harder to compensate

Maybe each "language"/"stat" levels independantly? e.g. you may be level 10 python and have unlocked everything but in java you're still level 2 and cant use loops.

This can also make it more educational where you "learn" new syntax of a language as you go!

and can make it fun for experienced players where they have to solve puzzles using limits or constraints
